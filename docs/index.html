Tampa Bay Nutrient Management Strategy

The Tampa Bay Estuary Program (TBEP) and the Tampa Bay Nitrogen Management Consortium (NMC) submitted the “Tampa Bay Watershed Management Summary” to the Florida Department of Environmental Protection (FDEP) on July 29, 2002. The purpose of that document (called the “2002 Tampa Bay Reasonable Assurance Document”) was to summarize the nitrogen management plan developed by the TBEP for Tampa Bay and to outline the underlying scientific basis for the plan. The document was formatted to facilitate its use in demonstrating reasonable assurance that designated uses of waterbody segments within the Tampa Bay basin, which were determined potentially impaired for nutrients pursuant to Chapter 62-303, F.A.C., will be maintained or restored. The document also provided a basis for establishment of alternative site-specific thresholds that more accurately reflect conditions beyond which an imbalance of flora and fauna may occur.

In November 2002, the FDEP Bureau of Watershed Management concluded that the “nitrogen management plan developed by TBEP for Tampa Bay provides reasonable assurance that impairment of designated uses related to nutrients in Tampa Bay will be adequately addressed.”

Subsequent to the 2002 effort, both a 2007 Reasonable Assurance Update and 2009 Reasonable Assurance Addendum were prepared for FDEP to ensure that the original 2002 determination would be extended until 2012. The 2007 Tampa Bay Update to Reasonable Assurance Documentation and 2009 Addendum were intended to: 1) provide an update on implementation of the Tampa Bay Nitrogen Management Strategy to FDEP for the 2003-2007 period; 2) provide adequate documentation to allow FDEP a finding of Reasonable Progress pursuant to rule 62-303.600, F.A.C. for the 2003-2007 period; and, 3) provide TN loading allocations to categories of nitrogen sources by major bay segment, and facility-specific and MS4 specific allocations within each major bay segment, to support any FDEP water quality based effluent limitation, FDEP Reasonable Assurance determination, and to comply with the federally-recognized TMDL for Tampa Bay. Subsequent Reasonable Assurance Updates were submitted in 2012, 2017 and 2022 to the FDEP to extend the Reasonable Assurance determination through 2026. As of the 2022 Reasonable Assurance Update, FDEP concluded the document “satisfies the requirements for continued approval of the RAP.” FDEP also concurred “that the proposed (NMC) corrective actions are reasonable and sufficient to address the issues in Old Tampa Bay” not meeting its chlorophyll-a numeric nutrient criteria in 4 of the 5 RAP years and requested periodic updates on corrective action implementation over the 2022-2026 period.

The format of this 2027 Reasonable Assurance (RA) Update follows that provided in the draft “Guidance for Development of Documentation to Provide Reasonable Assurance that Proposed Pollution Control Mechanisms will Result in the Restoration of Designated Uses in Impaired Waters” provided by FDEP in February 2002. For each element, updated 2027 Reasonable Assurance documentation is integrated with any relevant documentation detailed in the 2002, 2007, 2009 Addendum, 2012, 2017 and 2022 RA submittals.

Additional Information

This book is the web companion to the 2027 Reasonable Assurance (RA) Update prepared by the Tampa Bay Estuary Program (TBEP) and the Tampa Bay Nitrogen Management Consortium (NMC) for the Florida Department of Environmental Protection (FDEP). It presents the narrative, figures, and tables of the RA Update for the 2022–2026 reporting period, with each figure and table linked back to the analysis code that produces it in this repository, tbep-tech/reasonable-assurance-analysis.

NoteReproducibility

Figures and tables are generated programmatically, not hand-edited. Run R/dat_proc.R (data assembly) → R/figs.R (figures) and R/docs.R (tables). Loading inputs come from the companion tbep-tech/load-estimates pipeline; water-quality thresholds and segment averaging use the tbeptools R package. All historic and contemporary loading information is openly accessible at https://tbep.org/tampa-bay-nitrogen-loads.

Prior Reasonable Assurance Updates were submitted in 2002, 2007 (with a 2009 Addendum), 2012, 2017, and 2022. As of the 2022 RA Update, FDEP concluded the document “satisfies the requirements for continued approval of the RAP,” and concurred that the proposed NMC corrective actions were “reasonable and sufficient to address the issues in Old Tampa Bay,” requesting periodic updates on corrective-action implementation over the 2022–2026 period.

Headline finding for 2022–2026: FDEP-adopted chlorophyll-a thresholds were met in all major bay segments over the 2022–2026 RA period (see Overview, Figure 2). This represents a marked improvement in overall water quality, particularly in Old Tampa Bay (OTB), which did not meet its chlorophyll-a numeric nutrient criteria in four of the five years of the preceding (2017–2021) period.

Use the sidebar to navigate: the Overview chapter covers the waterbody, water-quality goals, nitrogen loading, and seagrass habitat; the Summary chapter covers corrective actions, progress since 2022, and the delivery-ratio table; and Provenance maps every deliverable to its source code.
