Appendices 1–5: Entity Load Assessments

Appendices 1–5 present the per-entity, per-facility allocation assessment for each bay segment over the 2022–2025 Reasonable Assurance period. For each entity the table lists the facility (point-source discharge or MS4), its allocation (percent of remaining load, where applicable, and TMDL-equivalent tons/yr), and the mean annual effective load over 2022–2025, with an allocation status. Each table is rendered natively from a per-segment CSV in data/.

Source. These are the “Assessment” tables generated by the TBEP load-reporting pipeline (show_aaassess(), from data/aa2225.RData) — the same tables published at https://loads.tbep.org/drafttables.html — covering 2022–2025. A facility whose effective load is at or below its allocated tons is within allocation; one above is flagged exceeds allocation. Per entity, the Total row compares the entity’s combined effective load to its combined allocation.

An “exceeds allocation” status for an individual facility does not by itself indicate a segment-level reasonable-assurance failure. Under the NMC framework (Tables 2–6, Section 2.d), entity/facility exceedances are evaluated (NMC Actions 4–5) only when a segment’s chlorophyll-a threshold is not met, and entity-generated reductions are retained as indefinite credits (2009 RA Addendum §IX.D). All segments met their chlorophyll-a thresholds over 2022–2026.
